Apply your icon

  1. to put the icon on your clipboard.
  2. In Finder, select the folder or app and choose File ▸ Get Info (⌘I).
  3. Click the small icon at the top-left of the Info window so it's highlighted.
  4. Press ⌘V. To undo later, select that icon again and press Delete.

Apps in /Applications may ask for your password. Some system apps can't be changed.
